# Non-Functional Requirements

## Platform

NFR-001

Windows 10 and Windows 11 support.

NFR-002

.NET 8 runtime.

---

## Performance

NFR-003

Support processing 15,000 to 30,000 invoice records.

NFR-004

Support 150 customer companies per batch.

NFR-005

Application startup time less than 5 seconds.

---

## Security

NFR-006

All invoice data must remain within local infrastructure.

NFR-007

No external AI services may be used.

NFR-008

Credentials shall be securely stored.

---

## Maintainability

NFR-009

Business rules shall be configurable.

NFR-010

Output naming rules shall be configurable.

NFR-011

AI configuration shall be configurable.

---

## Reliability

NFR-012

File generation success rate above 99%.

NFR-013

Failed records shall be logged.

NFR-014

System shall preserve source data.

---
